## Razorback game day rules that shape the order

The university sets the clock. On the Friday before a game, tents may rise at 5 p.m., and they have to be down by noon the day after [Source: policies.uark.edu, 2026]. Each tent measures 10 feet by 10 feet at most unless advance written permission says otherwise, and every tent stays weighted, never staked. Propane and covered charcoal grills are allowed, but not in parking garages. The university permits personal, non-commercial tailgating across campus on game day, and some spots need advance approval from Facilities Management. Off limits: student residential areas, intramural fields, and sidewalks.
